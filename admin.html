    How a flight change re-plans a shipment

    • DepartureCounter time = ETD − 4 h international, − 3 h domestic. Hub cut off = counter − line haul time − 30 min buffer. Pickup slot = hub cut off − captain run time. All three recompute on every ETD change.
    • ArrivalDriver at carousel = ETA + 35 min domestic, + 60 min international. Delivery ETA = that + drive time from live traffic.
    • Under 60 minRe-plan silently, tell the driver and customer new times.
    • Over 60 minRe-plan and ask ops to confirm if a slot or line haul changes. Customer told once confirmed.
    • CancelledHold bags at hub or counter, open exception, ask customer for the new PNR, re-price nothing.

    Who gets told, and when

    • CustomerNew pickup window or delivery ETA on WhatsApp, with the flight status line so they see why.
    • Pickup driverSlot moved in the app, run sheet re-ordered.
    • Delivery driverCarousel time moved, stop sequence re-planned.
    • HubCut off moved on the bay board.
    • OpsFeed line, and a decision card if the change breaks a line haul.
    • PartnerWebhook with the new times for OTA, hotel or airline.

    Data sources and limits

    • NowAviationStack for schedules, gates, delays; ADS-B for airborne position. Covers Indian carriers and international arrivals.
    • Cost₹8,000–15,000 a month at current volume; polling only inside the flight window keeps it there.
    • LaterAirport AODB feed (Adani, GMR) gives belt numbers and first-bag times; airline DCS confirms the bag is on the flight.
    • Honest limitPublic feeds lag airline systems 2–5 min and can miss gate changes. The buffers exist for that reason.

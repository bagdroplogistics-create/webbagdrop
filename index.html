How it works for a wedding

  • 1The hosts or the planner open the wedding with us. The planner can send the guest list as a sheet.
  • 2Every family gets a link to a page made for the wedding. About three minutes to book, no code, no payment.
  • 3Bags are tagged and sealed at the door, delivered to the room, and brought home after.
  • 4The hosts receive one statement. Guests never see a rate.
